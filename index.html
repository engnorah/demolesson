What Is Phishing?
Phishing
A social engineering attack where someone impersonates a trusted source to trick you into revealing sensitive information, credentials, or installing malware.
Four Common Types
01

Email Phishing

Mass, generic messages sent to many people at once.

02

Spear Phishing

Targeted at one person or organization, using real details.

03

Smishing

Delivered via SMS or text message.

04

Vishing

Delivered by phone call or voice message.

How To Stay Safe
Five Quick Wins
01
Verify the sender before you act — check the full email address, not just the display name.
02
Hover over links to preview the real destination before clicking.
03
Slow down — urgency and threats are a deliberate manipulation tactic.
04
Enable multi-factor authentication wherever it's available.
05
Report suspicious messages to IT/security — don't just delete them.
07 / 09
Phishing Or Not?
Quick Fire Round
1. Your "bank" texts asking you to confirm your PIN via a link.
Correct answer: Phishing — legitimate banks never ask you to confirm a PIN via link.
2. A colleague calls asking you to urgently transfer funds — from an unfamiliar number.
Correct answer: Phishing — a social engineering / vishing pattern. Verify via a known number first.
3. An email from IT asks you to reset your password through your company's normal portal.
Correct answer: Not Phishing — routine resets through your normal, known portal are expected. No urgency, no external link.
